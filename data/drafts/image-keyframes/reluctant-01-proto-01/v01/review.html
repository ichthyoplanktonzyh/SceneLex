  • 这是区分 reluctant 与普通慢动作的判据帧, 结果是本批最严重的失败。
  • 要求'手停在离叉子约三分之二处且没有碰到叉子'; 实际手已握叉、叉齿已插入西兰花。

Semantic purpose: 把 reluctant 与普通服从分开的那一帧。从“后靠”直接过渡到“握着叉子”，插值与视频模型都会生成一次平滑、正常的伸手，中途那半秒的停顿会被抹掉——而启动延迟正是不情愿的核心证据。身体没有跟上手，也让“只有手在配合”直接可见。
Must show
  • Hand stopped short of the fork and not touching it.
  • Torso still back — the body has not committed to the action.
  • Gaze still not on the plate.
Must avoid
  • The hand reaching for anything other than the fork.
  • The hand recoiling backwards, which would read as refuse.
  • A fast, confident reach with the body following it.

semantic_climax
semantic: weakcontinuity: weakcomposition: passcharacter: fail
  • 本批语义最强的一张: 西兰花已在叉上、停在嘴边、嘴仍闭合, '动作几乎完成但尚未完成'的停顿确实可读。
  • 没有出现 gagging、干呕、闭眼或母亲喂食等 must_avoid 项。
  • 视线转向母亲, 而 v02 明确要求看着西兰花或垂下——转向母亲会把这一刻读成征求许可, 不是自己承担的迟疑。
  • 躯干没有后靠, 'only the arm has committed' 未成立。
Semantic purpose: 全场最强的一帧：动作已经几乎完成，阻力仍然在。第一次停顿只能证明“启动慢”，第二次停顿证明阻力贯穿整个执行过程，这正是本场景要教的判据。缺了它，整条弧线塌成“犹豫一下然后正常吃”。同时它把“孩子自己把食物送到嘴边”钉住，与 refuse 划清界限。
Must show
  • Broccoli on the fork, held still just short of the mouth.
  • Mouth closed — a visible halt, not a bite or a swallow in progress.
  • Body still withdrawn while only the arm has committed.
  • No one else's hand on the fork or on the child — he is doing this himself.
Must avoid
  • Gagging, retching, or turning the head away from the food at the mouth.
  • Eyes squeezed shut, which would read as fear rather than low willingness.
  • The mother feeding him or steadying his arm.

Must show
  • Fork put down on the plate — the action was completed.
  • Body still reclined, matching the withdrawal established in shot-02.
  • Flat mouth, gaze low or averted.
  • No reach for a second piece.
Must avoid
  • Smiling, nodding, or leaning forward for another piece.
  • Spitting the food out or a face that reads as revulsion at the taste.
  • Pushing the plate away.
