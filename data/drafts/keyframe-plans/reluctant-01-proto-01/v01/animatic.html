shot-010.0s – 2.5s (2.5s) · establish_trigger · audio: optional_dialogue

shot-01-kf-010.0s → 1.0s · hold 1.0s
initial
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERTwo-shot at the dining table. The child sits upright, both hands flat on the table, head facing forward — not looking at the plate. The mother is turned toward him, her near hand lifted off the table and starting to move toward the plate; her index finger is not yet aimed at the broccoli. The plate of broccoli and the fork sit in the middle foreground between them, untouched.
Why this frame: 锚定“请求尚未成立”的起点：孩子的注意力还不在盘子上，身体也还没有任何反应。缺了它，下一帧“妈妈指着、孩子看向盘子”就无法证明注意力转移是被请求引发的，孩子随后的停顿也可能被读成他本来就在发呆。
  • Child's gaze directed forward, away from the plate.
  • Mother's hand already in motion but not yet pointing.
  • Plate and fork within the child's easy reach, with nothing blocking access to them.
shot-01-kf-021.0s → 2.5s · hold 1.5s
triggerfinal
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERThe mother's arm is fully extended, index finger pointing at the broccoli on the child's plate; her eyes are on the child while she holds the gesture. The child has turned his head down toward the plate. His hands are still flat on the table and his posture is unchanged — upright, no withdrawal yet.
Why this frame: 同一张画面同时是本镜头的触发事件与结束状态：请求关系成立（指向 + 注视 + 保持等待），孩子的注意力已经落到盘子上，但身体尚未做出任何回应。它是 shot-02 的全部阻力得以被读成“对这个请求的回应”的前提。把“请求发出”与“孩子看过去”拆成两帧只会得到两张几乎相同的图，所以这里用一帧承担两个角色。
  • Pointing finger clearly aimed at the broccoli on the child's plate.
  • Mother's gaze on the child while the gesture is held — the waiting is what makes it a request.
  • Child's gaze now on the plate.
  • Child's body still neutral: hands on the table, no lean, no resistance yet.

shot-022.5s – 7.3s (4.8s) · semantic_climax · audio: sfx

shot-02-kf-012.5s → 3.3s · hold 0.8s
initial
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERTighter framing on the child, chest up, with the plate and fork in the lower half of the frame. He is in exactly the posture that ended shot-01 — upright, hands flat on the table, eyes down on the broccoli, shoulders slightly tense. Nothing has moved.
Why this frame: 跨剪辑点的连续性锚点，同时是“什么都没发生”本身的证据。删掉它，画面就从两人镜头直接跳进“已经后靠”的状态，观众失去衡量停顿长度的基准——而停顿是不情愿最先可见的形态。
  • Posture identical to the end of shot-01: hands flat, upright, gaze on the plate.
  • Fork within easy reach and untouched.
  • No external obstacle and no one restraining the child.
shot-02-kf-023.3s → 4.3s · hold 1.0s
trigger
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERThe child has leaned back a few degrees, shoulders settling against the chair back, hands still on the table but drawn slightly in toward his own body. His head is turned away from the plate; his eyes are directed to the side or down at the table edge — not at the food and not at the mother.
Why this frame: 抵抗第一次变得可见：后撤 + 视线回避。这一帧把“停顿”与“走神”区分开——身体正在离开被要求的对象。缺了它，动作会从中立坐姿直接跳到伸手，整段就只剩下“慢”，而慢本身不是不情愿。
  • Torso withdrawn from the table — small but unmistakable.
  • Gaze aimed away from the plate.
  • Hands drawn back slightly rather than reaching.
  • Neutral mouth.
shot-02-kf-034.3s → 5.5s · hold 1.2s
transition
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERThe reach is halted mid-way. The child's arm is extended roughly two thirds of the distance to the fork, hand open, hovering above the table without touching it. His torso has not come forward with the arm — he is still leaned back — and his gaze is still off the plate. The arm is the only thing that has moved.
Why this frame: 把 reluctant 与普通服从分开的那一帧。从“后靠”直接过渡到“握着叉子”，插值与视频模型都会生成一次平滑、正常的伸手，中途那半秒的停顿会被抹掉——而启动延迟正是不情愿的核心证据。身体没有跟上手，也让“只有手在配合”直接可见。
  • Hand stopped short of the fork and not touching it.
  • Torso still back — the body has not committed to the action.
  • Gaze still not on the plate.
shot-02-kf-045.5s → 6.5s · hold 1.0s
semantic_climax
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERThe child holds the fork with a piece of broccoli speared on it, raised and stopped just short of his mouth. His lips are closed, not yet parted. His torso is still slightly back, so the arm has had to travel further to bridge the gap; his eyes are on the broccoli or lowered, not turned toward the mother.
Why this frame: 全场最强的一帧：动作已经几乎完成，阻力仍然在。第一次停顿只能证明“启动慢”，第二次停顿证明阻力贯穿整个执行过程，这正是本场景要教的判据。缺了它，整条弧线塌成“犹豫一下然后正常吃”。同时它把“孩子自己把食物送到嘴边”钉住，与 refuse 划清界限。
  • Broccoli on the fork, held still just short of the mouth.
  • Mouth closed — a visible halt, not a bite or a swallow in progress.
  • Body still withdrawn while only the arm has committed.
  • No one else's hand on the fork or on the child — he is doing this himself.
shot-02-kf-056.5s → 7.3s · hold 0.8s
final
TEXTUAL PLACEHOLDERThe bite is over. The fork is back down on the plate and the child's hands have returned toward the table. His mouth is closed and his expression is flat, jaw at rest or on a last slow chew. He is still sitting slightly back in the chair, gaze low and away — he has not leaned in toward the plate.
Why this frame: 证明两件事同时成立：动作确实发生并由孩子自己完成（不是 refuse），而意愿始终没有转正（不是主动配合）。动作完成后身体仍维持后撤姿态，是“低意愿贯穿到底”的收尾证据。咀嚼没有单独成帧——它与这一帧的差别只是下颌位置，本身不改变任何语义推断。
  • Fork put down on the plate — the action was completed.
  • Body still reclined, matching the withdrawal established earlier.
  • Flat mouth, gaze low or averted.
